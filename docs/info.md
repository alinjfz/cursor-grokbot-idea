​🧠 Why This Hackathon?

User Uploaded Image
​Bots have changed the interface.

​An AI bot can understand an objective, search across systems, use tools, interact with websites, make decisions and execute tasks.

​And that raises some very interesting questions:

​What does a storefront look like when the customer is an AI?

​How does a merchant sell to a bot rather than a person?

​Can a bot negotiate a purchase?

​Can bots discover and transact with other bots?

​How should humans authorize autonomous purchases?

​What happens to search when bots decide what products to show?

​What does customer loyalty mean when your bot chooses where you buy?

​How do we make autonomous commerce trustworthy and observable?

​What entirely new businesses become possible?

​This hackathon is about actioning those answers.

​💡 Why Participate?
​Ship something real with Origin, Grok Bots, and Cursor.

​Build on real commerce infrastructure Shopify, Supabase, and modern commerce APIs rather than building everything from scratch.

​Build alongside London's developers, founders, AI engineers, designers and investors.

​Show what bots can actually do. Win prizes, sponsor challenges, swag and recognition — and present your product live to an expert judging panel.
