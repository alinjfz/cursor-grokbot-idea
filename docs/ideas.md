IDEA 6 — YOU’RE STOCK
The 5-second pitch: A buyer bot makes a cash offer for the jacket the judge is wearing.

The magic moment: Second 0: webcam on the judge. Second 3: a single line appears, Navy jacket, I’ll take it for £40, pickup now. Second 6: they say “no.” The bid becomes £55. Second 9: they step out of frame. The offer withdraws itself: Item left the building.

The trick: We do not identify the brand. Vision returns color plus garment type, which it is actually good at. The bid is a template. “Left the building” reuses the in-frame check: no jacket, no listing.

Why it feels new: Listings are something you create. Here the merchant is a person who walked into the camera, and delisting is walking away.

What we actually build: One webcam, one caption call, one offer line, one raise on “no,” one withdrawal when the garment leaves the frame.

Hackathon technology used: Camera and a vision model.

Build difficulty: Low

Demo risk: It names the garment wrong, or prices a lanyard. Constrain the prompt to outerwear only, and demo on whoever is wearing the clearest jacket.

If it works: People will remember being offered money for their own coat.

Designed reaction: “That’s hilarious.”
